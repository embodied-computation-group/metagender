::: legend
*Note.* Calibration is the self-estimated minus the actual per cent correct, in percentage points, in the discovery sample (N = 317). The gender × time contrast is the post-minus-pre change in the gender difference; a negative value means women revised their self-estimate further downward than men. Domain-general rows are the mean of the per-domain effects. Contrasts are women minus men, given as the posterior mean with its 95% highest-density interval (HDI). BF10 is the Savage–Dickey Bayes factor against a point null; Bayes factors in bold mark at least moderate evidence (BF10 above 3 for a difference, below 1/3 for no difference). % in ROPE is the share of the posterior inside the region of practical equivalence. The ROPE is ±0.1 SD of the outcome.
:::
