*Note.* Outcomes are participant-level posterior means from the unified hierarchical model, regressed on within-domain z-scored baseline calibration and pre→post update, with gender as a moderator. BF10 is the Savage–Dickey Bayes factor against a point null; Bayes factors in bold mark at least moderate evidence (BF10 above 3 for a difference, below 1/3 for no difference). % in ROPE is the share of the posterior inside the region of practical equivalence.
